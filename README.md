## ✨ Features

### 🖱️ RMB Tweak
The classic right-click drag — but better. If you drag over a slot multiple times, an item will be placed there multiple times. Replaces the standard Minecraft mechanic.

**Use case:** Quickly fill a crafting grid with the same item across all slots.

### 🖱️ LMB Tweak (with item)
Pick up or move items of the same type with a simple left-click drag.

**Use case:** Pick up all your cobblestone from a chest by dragging across it. Hold Shift and drag to shift-click everything into your inventory.

### 🖱️ LMB Tweak (without item)
Hold Shift and left-click drag to quickly move items into another inventory.

**Use case:** Empty a chest into your inventory by dragging across slots with Shift held.

### 🖱️ Wheel Tweak
Scroll to move items between inventories instantly.

**Use case:** Scroll down on a stack to move items one by one to your inventory. Scroll up to pull items from your inventory into a chest.

**Scroll direction options:**
- **Default:** Scroll up = pull items, scroll down = push items
- **Inverted:** Opposite of default
- **Inventory Position Aware:** Smart direction based on where your cursor is
